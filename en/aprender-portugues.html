Learn Portuguese

Speaking Portuguese helps you find work, use public services and meet people. There are free in-person options in São Paulo and materials to study online.

In person in São Paulo

Portas Abertas: Portuguese for Immigrants

A free public course run by São Paulo City Hall in participating municipal schools, at basic, intermediate and advanced levels. Enrollment does not depend on your immigration or document status.

Offered by
São Paulo City Hall (Education and Human Rights departments)
Cost
Free
Format
In person, at municipal schools
For
Adult immigrants. In 2023 the minimum age was 18: check the current rule
Checked on
26 Oct 2026

Official Portas Abertas page (in Portuguese)

How to enroll: go directly to the nearest participating school. In 2023, City Hall asked for an identity document (Brazilian or from your country) and proof of address; if you do not have proof of address, you can fill in a residence declaration. To find out which schools take part today, call 156 (City Hall service line, in Portuguese).

Tips for practicing

  • Start with everyday phrases: greeting people, asking for directions, saying your name and address.
  • Listen to radio, music or videos in Portuguese, even if you do not understand everything.
  • Talk to neighbors and coworkers. Making mistakes is part of learning.
