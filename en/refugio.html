Refuge

If you left your country because of persecution, war or serious human rights violations, you may have the right to apply for refuge in Brazil.

What to know

  • Refuge applications are assessed by the Federal Government. Only the responsible authority decides whether an application is accepted.
  • Applying for refuge is free.
  • Get guidance before applying. The organizations below can help.
