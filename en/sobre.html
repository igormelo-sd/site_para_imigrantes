About the project

First Steps in Brazil is a university extension project with an educational purpose.

Who made it

Igor Martins de Melo, a Computer Science student at FAM, as an extension activity on the topic of Migrants and Refugees.

Goal

To help people who have just arrived in Brazil find reliable sources, guidance services and places to learn Portuguese, in plain language and easy to use on a phone.

What this site is not

  • It is not an official service and has no partnership with the organizations mentioned.
  • It does not provide individual legal advice.
  • It does not decide on or speed up document or refuge applications.

Sources and review

The information points to official pages of the Federal Government, São Paulo City Hall, UNHCR and Missão Paz. Each service shows the date it was checked. The local coverage is the city of São Paulo.

Languages

The site is available in Portuguese, English and Spanish. The English and Spanish versions have not yet been reviewed by native speakers.

Privacy

This site has no sign-up, forms or tracking tools. It does not collect any information about visitors.
