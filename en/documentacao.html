Documents

Migration and refuge are different situations, with different procedures. The documents you need depend on your situation.

Before you start

  • There is no single list of documents that applies to everyone.
  • Get guidance from an official service or a support organization before starting an application.
  • Be wary of anyone who charges money to "guarantee" documents or deadlines.

Your rights

Brazil's Migration Law (Law No. 13,445 of 2017) guarantees migrants access to public health, education and social assistance services, under the same conditions as Brazilians.
