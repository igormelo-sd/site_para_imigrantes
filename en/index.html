Guidance for people who have just arrived in Brazil

Find official sources about documents and refuge, services that can guide you and places to learn Portuguese. Choose the topic you need.

  • Documents Where to find information about documents to live and work in Brazil.
  • Refuge Information for refugees and for people who want to apply for refuge.
  • Support in São Paulo Services that assist immigrants in the city of São Paulo.
